## What's in it

- **Thesis:** for most ads (simple UGC cuts, captions, B-roll, up to ~1 minute), editing in Claude Code beats editing in a timeline. Not claimed for long or high-production edits. The step change came with Astra and then Opus 5.5.
- **Examples shown:** three ads edited from raw footage in Claude Code (a UGC restaurant-finance ad, a hair-loss helmet ad, an AI animation ad made with the AI animation skill + Higgsfield MCP). Team reports 60–70% less edit time.
- **Beginner:** give Claude Code the brief (Notion) and the footage folder (Drive, or any asset tool with an MCP) and ask it to edit. No editing MCPs; Opus 5.5 medium edits natively. Also: generating B-roll, making variations from the brief, QA'ing creator submissions and edits.
- **Intermediate:** turn edits into skills. Feed in feedback and editing principles; Alex used the Frame MCP to compile 30 days of team feedback into the skill. Have editors narrate their edits on screen recordings to train skills. Share skills across the team (Parker brain, GitHub). Split into per-style skills (AI animation, mashup, UGC, ugly organic no captions) trained on 3–4 examples each.
- **Advanced:** a local review app, an internal Frame where the agent edits, takes timestamped notes and re-renders. Generally applicable notes write back to one editing-standards file that every skill reads, so skills improve with use. "Build feedback loops into anything you build."
- **Q&A:** editors aren't cooked; upskill them into strategists / skill builders, and good editors are still needed for QC and skill building. Roll out top-down (founders → lead → senior → junior editors). Tools mentioned but not properly tested: Claude Motion, HyperFrames (little difference vs his own skill), a ChatGPT plugin Orin John recommends. Follow: Orin John (Orin Meets World). Parker customers will get the video editing skills in the brain launch this week or next.
